Not connected Alerts work in ShuleHub; SMS needs an Africa's Talking account.

  1. Create an account at africastalking.com and top up some SMS credit.
  2. Copy your username and create an API key in your account settings.
  3. Where ShuleHub is hosted (for example Render, under Environment) add: AT_USERNAME = your username, and AT_API_KEY = your key. Optionally AT_SENDER_ID = your approved sender name.
  4. Restart ShuleHub, come back here and press Send a test SMS to my number.

Want to try the screens first? Set PAGER_SMS_MODE = test. SMS is then recorded but not really sent.
