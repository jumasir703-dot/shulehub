See how ShuleHub works

Open any part of the app with a pretend school, Mwanga Academy, already filled in: teachers, classes, a finished timetable, students, exam results, attendance and fees. Click around, add things, delete things. Your own school's records are never touched and nothing you do in the example is kept.

Timetable

Set up your grades, subjects, teachers, rooms and school day once. The auto-scheduler then fills the whole week for every class without clashing a teacher or a room.

  • 3 classes with a complete weekly timetable
  • 8 teachers, each with their own timetable
  • Export to PDF for the notice board, or CSV

Exam Analysis

Enter or import marks and get merit lists, subject and stream analysis, teacher performance, exam-to-exam comparison and printable report forms.

  • 3 exams for each of two grades, 8 subjects each
  • Rankings, grade distributions and improvement over time
  • A report form for any student

Students & Guardians

One record for every learner: admission details, guardians and their phone numbers, and enrolment status, with promotion at the end of the year.

  • 42 students across 3 classes
  • Guardian contacts and class lists
  • Search, import and export

Attendance

Teachers mark the class register in a few clicks. See who is absent today, which children keep missing school, and reports per class.

  • Three weeks of registers already marked
  • Absentee list with guardian phone numbers
  • Per-class attendance reports

Fees & Payments

Set the fee structure per grade, create every invoice in one click, record payments (cash, M-Pesa, bank) and print receipts and statements.

  • A full term of invoices with payments recorded
  • Balances: who has cleared and who still owes
  • PDF receipts and fee statements

The example uses made-up names and phone numbers. Leaving it, or closing ShuleHub, discards everything in it.
