Get the ShuleHub app

Use ShuleHub like an app on your phone, or download the offline version for a Windows computer.

On your phone

No app store needed. It opens full-screen from your home screen, like any other app.

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It must be opened in Safari for this to work.

ShuleHub is already installed on this device.
